Step 2 · Prepare

Before you continue

Use these prompts to prepare for checking the official requirements. This is not a definitive document list.

  • Confirm the issuing authority for your state or district.
  • Read the current requirements on the official portal.
  • Check whether supporting documents or a fee are required.
  • Keep only relevant information ready; do not share sensitive data here.
Checklist status: illustrative. Verify every requirement with the official authority before applying.
Step 3 · Official hand-off

Continue on the official portal

GovEase does not issue certificates or submit applications. The relevant government department or state portal is responsible for authoritative requirements and processing.

No external application link is embedded in this demo because the correct destination depends on your state and service.

Design intent

Clarity before action.

GovEase is a proposed orientation layer—not a replacement for official portals such as UMANG, DigiLocker, or state/department websites.

What this concept prioritizes

  • Everyday task language instead of department jargon.
  • Visible jurisdiction and source transparency.
  • Short, scannable preparation prompts.
  • A clear boundary between guidance and official processing.

What to test with users

Can a person find a relevant service, explain what it is for, understand that requirements vary, and identify where official processing happens?

Accessibility note: keyboard-focus styles, semantic controls, readable contrast, and responsive layout are included in this prototype. A full WCAG audit has not been conducted.
